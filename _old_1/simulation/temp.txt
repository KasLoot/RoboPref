You are working in the RoboPref repository. Your job is to implement and run a
data-collection suite in MuJoCo for three VLA training dataset categories. Work in
phases with the gates described below; do not start the full collection run without
passing the smoke gate.

## Existing code (read all four files first — they work, and they encode tuning)

- `simulation/ik.py` — `DiffIK`, damped-least-squares differential IK for the ARX L5
  (6 arm joints, TCP offset on `link6`, quats are wxyz). **Frozen: do not modify.**
  Read the `step()` docstring carefully: the control loop must integrate the
  *command* (`q_cmd = ik.step(...); data.ctrl[:6] = q_cmd`) and feed the returned
  command back in. Never rebuild the setpoint from measured `qpos` — that folds
  gravity sag back into the loop and leaves centimeters of residual.
- `simulation/servo.py` — `CriticalDamper`; call `damper.apply(data)` every step
  before `mj_step`. ζ = 1.0 is measured tuning. **Frozen: do not modify or retune.**
- `simulation/engine.py` — reference for the servo loop structure (`servo_to`),
  waypoint settling (POS_TOL / ORI_TOL / HOLD / TIMEOUT), and marker drawing. Reuse
  the pattern, not the file. Keep `engine.py` working.
- `simulation/camera.py` — Tk preview canvas; cameras `"third_person"` and
  `"wrist_cam"`; offscreen framebuffer is 640×480. The canvas is for interactive
  preview only — collection must be headless with its own `mujoco.Renderer`s and
  must never call `time.sleep`.
- Scene: `simulation/assets/robots/arx_l5/scene.xml`, keyframe `"home"`. The scene
  path is currently hardcoded in `engine.py`; put shared paths/constants in a small
  config module rather than editing `ik.py`/`servo.py`.

## Phase 0 — discover, don't assume (print a model report before writing task code)

Inspect the compiled model and print:
1. `model.opt.timestep` (expected 0.002 → 500 Hz proprio; whatever it is, record at
   every physics step and store the actual rate — do not resample).
2. The gripper actuator: the actuator that does not drive `joint1..joint6` — its
   name, ctrl range, driven joint(s), and finger opening width. Derive open/closed
   ctrl values from the range.
3. A tool-down grasp orientation: the world quaternion that points the TCP axis
   (+x of `link6`, per `TCP_OFFSET`) along −z world. Derive it from the home pose
   and verify with a short `servo_to`-style test before trusting it.
4. Reachable workspace: probe with IK servoing to place the two mats where a
   tool-down grasp works across their full extent. Pick block size to fit the
   gripper (start at 0.035 m cubes; adjust from the measured opening).

If anything makes the spec impossible (e.g., gripper cannot span any reasonable
block), stop and ask. Otherwise decide, document in README, and proceed.

## Phase 1 — scene builder

Build scenes programmatically (prefer `mujoco.MjSpec`; XML string templating is an
acceptable fallback) and recompile per episode — do not try to mutate one static
model. Every episode's scene contains:

- White mat (left) and black mat (right): thin colored box geoms, both fully inside
  the verified workspace.
- Cross marker: two thin bright geoms flush on the white mat forming a "+", center
  sampled uniformly per episode from a centered square region (side ≈ 0.10 m) of
  the white mat.
- Blocks: free-joint cubes, colors from {red, green, blue, yellow} with clearly
  distinct RGBA; mass and friction tuned until grasping and 4-high stacking are
  reliable (raise friction / adjust condim if blocks slip in the smoke test).
- Spawn helpers: N blocks at random non-overlapping poses on the black mat (center
  gap ≥ 1.5 × block size, margin from mat edges); for pre-built stacks, spawn the
  stack at the cross, settle 0.5 s, verify it is intact and in place, else resample.
- Determinism: `seed = f(category, task_id, episode_id)` with a stable documented
  formula; use `np.random.default_rng(seed)`; failed episodes retry with a derived
  seed (`seed + 10_000 * attempt`), never silent reuse.

## Phase 2 — scripted expert

Primitives built on `DiffIK` + `CriticalDamper`, headless, mirroring
`engine.servo_to` (settling via POS_TOL/ORI_TOL/HOLD, per-waypoint timeout):

- `pick(block)`: pregrasp above the block (tool-down, z clearance) → descend to
  grasp height → close gripper (fixed duration or until settled) → lift.
- `place(target_xy, target_z)`: transit above → descend → open → retreat. Looser
  orientation tolerance during transit is fine; tight at grasp/place.
- Targets from ground truth: mat placement = cross center at z = half block height;
  stack placement = current stack-top center at z = top + block size. Re-read block
  poses from `data` after every placement (earlier placements shift things).
- Success predicates (ground truth, evaluated after a 1.0 s settle):
  base block within 2 cm (xy) of the cross; stacked block within 2 cm (xy) of the
  block below and within z tolerance; block linear speed < 1 cm/s; full-task
  success = every placement succeeded ∧ executed order = intended order.
- Failure policy: any timeout or failed predicate → discard the recording, resample
  the episode with a derived seed, count it. If a task's failure rate exceeds 20%,
  abort that task and report — do not grind out a bad expert.

## Phase 3 — recorder (HDF5 schema — follow exactly)

All datasets chunked and compressed (lzf or gzip). Proprio and actions logged every
physics step; cameras captured whenever `sim_time >= next 1/30 s tick` (nominal
30 Hz — timestamps are authoritative, do not claim exact rates).

```
/proprio/t                  (T,)   f64  sim time, s
/proprio/joint_pos          (T,6)  f32  rad
/proprio/joint_vel          (T,6)  f32  rad/s
/proprio/joint_torque       (T,6)  f32  data.qfrc_actuator at the arm dofs
/proprio/ee_pos             (T,3)  f32  TCP world position (DiffIK.tool_pose)
/proprio/ee_quat_wxyz       (T,4)  f32
/actions/t                  (T,)   f64
/actions/q_cmd              (T,6)  f32  the setpoints actually written to data.ctrl
/actions/gripper            (T,)   f32  gripper ctrl value
/cameras/<name>/t           (K,)   f64      for <name> in {third_person, wrist_cam}
/cameras/<name>/rgb         (K,480,640,3) u8
/cameras/<name>/depth       (K,480,640)   u16  millimeters, 0 = invalid
```

- Render with dedicated `mujoco.Renderer`s at 640×480 (one for RGB, one with
  `enable_depth_rendering()`, or toggle a single renderer). Never use the Tk canvas
  during collection. Convert depth meters → uint16 mm (clip, 0 for invalid). This
  uint16 choice is a deliberate storage decision — document it in the README.
- Root attrs: `category, task_id, episode_id, instruction` (verbatim string used),
  `seed, success` (True for every kept episode), `executed_order` (e.g.
  `"red,green,blue"` bottom→top; single color for Category C), `blocks_present`,
  `init_stack` (bottom→top colors, empty if none), `split` (`"val"` for every 10th
  episode of a task, else `"train"`), `sim_timestep`, `tcp_offset`,
  `quat_convention="wxyz"`, per-camera intrinsics (fovy, resolution) and extrinsics
  at episode start.

## Phase 4 — task definitions (budgets are fixed; do not change them)

**Category A — `stacking_blocks_ambiguous`** (task 1). Instruction (all episodes):
"Stack the blocks from the black mat to the white mat at the cross position."
Blocks: red, green, blue. 240 episodes = 40 × each of the 6 stacking orders,
interleaved so a partial run stays balanced. The order lives only in
`executed_order`, never in the instruction.

**Category B — `stacking_blocks_ordered`** (tasks 1–4). Trained orders:
red-green-blue, blue-green-red, green-red-blue, red-blue-green. Held out (zero
episodes, record in scene.yaml): blue-red-green, green-blue-red. Instruction:
"Stack the blocks from the black mat to the white mat at the cross position, with
order from bottom to top of {c1}, {c2}, {c3} blocks." 60 episodes per task.

**Category C — `stacking_blocks_decomposed`** (tasks 1–8), colors
{red, green, blue, yellow}.
- Tasks 1–4 (T-MAT, one per color): "Place the {color} block on the white mat at
  the cross position." Initial state: no stack; 2–4 blocks (random subset always
  including {color}) on the black mat. 60 episodes per task.
- Tasks 5–8 (T-TOP, one per color): "Place the {color} block on top of the stack
  at the cross position." Initial state: pre-built stack at the cross of height
  h ∈ {1,2,3}, balanced round-robin (40 episodes per height); stack colors sampled
  without replacement from the other three colors; {color} plus 0–2 distractors on
  the black mat. 120 episodes per task.

Each category's `scene.yaml`: scene description, instruction templates, task ids
and budgets, randomization ranges, seed formula, split rule, trained/held-out
orders (B), schema version.

## Phase 5 — CLI, gates, and the run

`collect.py`:
```
uv run python simulation/collect/collect.py \
    --category {a,b,c,all} [--task N] [--episodes N] [--root PATH] \
    [--preview] [--overwrite]
```
- Resumable: skip existing episode files that pass a fast integrity check;

- Progress bar with per-task counts, running failure rate, and ETA.

shapes/dtypes, monotonic timestamps, camera-timestamp alignment (each camera t
within one physics step of some proprio t), `success == True`, `executed_order`
consistent with gripper open/close events; balance checks (A: 40/order; C T-TOP:
40/height); 3 PNG contact sheets per task (first/mid/last frames, both cameras);
summary table.

## Ground rules

- `ik.py` and `servo.py` are frozen; `damper.apply(data)` precedes every `mj_step`;
  q_cmd is always integrated, never rebuilt from measured qpos.
- Deterministic given seeds; every kept episode has `success=True`; discarded
  attempts are counted and reported, never written.
- Decide-and-document over ask, except where the model makes the spec impossible.
- Total scope: 240 + 240 + 720 = 1,200 episodes ≈ 2,160 placements. If projected
  storage exceeds ~1 TB or wallclock exceeds ~12 h at the smoke gate, report and
  wait rather than proceeding.
